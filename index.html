What We Do

Fire & Security Services

From new installations to ongoing maintenance — specialist services delivered to British Standards across Scotland.

Fire Alarm Installation

Full design and installation of fire detection and alarm systems to BS5839 Part 1 for commercial premises and Part 6 for residential.

  • Conventional & addressable systems
  • L1–L5 and M systems
  • New builds & retrofits
  • Full documentation provided
  • Hochiki, Advanced, Notifier & more

Commissioning

Thorough programming and testing of fire and security systems to ensure full compliance with current standards — protecting life and property.

  • Full system testing & sign-off
  • Cause & effect programming
  • Zone mapping & detector testing
  • Compliance certificates issued
  • Handover training for staff

Ongoing Maintenance

Regular servicing and testing keeps your system compliant, reliable and ready when it matters most. Flexible maintenance contracts available.

  • 6-monthly & annual service contracts
  • Weekly & monthly user testing support
  • Priority fault response
  • Service reports & records
  • Insurance-compliant documentation

Fault Finding & Repairs

Specialist diagnostic skills developed over 30+ years — called in by major companies when their own engineers can't resolve a fault.

  • Intermittent fault diagnosis
  • Panel faults & resets
  • False alarm reduction
  • System upgrades & expansions
  • Emergency callouts available
